## Diligence Questions

- What system bottleneck is measured, and under which production workload?
- Which claims are independently verified versus company-reported?
- What are the architecture, node, packaging, memory, and interconnect dependencies?
- How mature are the compiler, runtime, frameworks, debugging, and observability tools?
- What changes are required in the customer's application or infrastructure?
- What are tapeout, yield, qualification, supply-chain, and second-source risks?
- What performance, power, cost, utilization, and reliability metrics matter to buyers?
- What incumbent roadmap or internal customer alternative could erase the advantage?
