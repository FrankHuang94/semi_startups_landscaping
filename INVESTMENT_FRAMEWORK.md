# Investment Framework

## Screening Dimensions

Companies are scored from 1 to 5 on market size, timing, technical differentiation,
founder quality, customer pull, business model quality, capital efficiency, competitive
intensity, exit potential, and strategic scarcity. A score is optional until evidence
supports it. For competitive intensity, 5 means favorable or manageable competition.

## Priority States

- `pass`: Thesis conflict or unattractive risk-adjusted outcome.
- `monitor`: Relevant but insufficient evidence or timing.
- `watchlist`: Credible opportunity requiring periodic tracking.
- `active_diligence`: Current workstream with owners and open questions.
- `high_conviction`: Strong evidence across technical, commercial, and return dimensions.

## Technical Diligence

Assess architecture, process node, packaging, memory hierarchy, interconnect, software,
compiler/runtime maturity, benchmark quality, manufacturing dependencies, supply-chain
risk, and customer integration friction. Normalize claims against the workload and total
system context rather than relying on peak specifications.

## Venture Return Test

Evaluate whether the company can reach a venture-scale outcome after accounting for
capital required, dilution, time to qualification, gross margin, customer concentration,
incumbent response, and plausible exit multiples. Strategic relevance is not by itself
evidence of venture-scale returns.

## Exit Analysis

Map likely acquirers, historical transactions, buyer portfolio gaps, build-versus-buy
behavior, and IPO comparables. Treat undisclosed transaction values as unknown and avoid
back-solving valuations without a documented methodology.
